# Dark Diviner Fx Timer

## What it does

Removes most of the effects from Vortex and replaces the center of Vortex and the overhead ball from Thunderblight with a timer. Reduces the amount of extra particles in most Dark Diviner skills.

Originally seen on the [Erinn World forums](https://erinn.world/forum.php?mod=viewthread&tid=23207&extra=&page=1). I found it in an unusable state, reversed it, and significantly modified it myself.

### How it's made

Swap some of the swirly animations out for the timer images.
