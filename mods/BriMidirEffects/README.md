# Bri Midir Effects

## What it does

Removes the majority of Midir's effects to reduce lag. Changes the colors and size of some telegraphs to improve visual clarity. This makes it much easier to see, rather than everything just being red on red on red.

The laser aiming telegraph is now larger and green for both halo links and the multi lasers he does at random. Midir's multi laser now transition from green for 3 seconds to yellow for 1 to red for 1 before firing.
His spin in place feather shot telegraph replaces the tiny white fathers with extremely long pink ones.
The feathers he tosses out from his swipes are blue on even numbered swings.
The circles for 60% have their effects reduced and walls cut shorter.
The tiny red line that telegraphs his jump is now a large purple cone with an up arrow over his head.
The tiny blue line that telegraphs his backdrop is now a large yellow cone.
The blue line at 30% is thicker.
Reduced number of particles that make up the 65% and 50% orbs in g4 to greatly reduce lag.
The blue light on the double laser telegraph much easier to see.
Tornado effects replaced with a cage to more easily see borders and reduce lag.

### How it's made

Through long and painful trial and error. See the file itself for some notes in the comments.
