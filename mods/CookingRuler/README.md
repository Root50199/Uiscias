# Cooking Ruler

## What it does

Expands the cooking bar and adds and overlay with tick marks every 1%.

Heavily modified, but originally from [this Erinn World thread](https://www.erinn.world/forum.php?mod=viewthread&tid=16795&extra=page%3D2&page=1) by 隐翅.

### How it's made

The ruler was made in paint.net and the size and position of the gauge was edited in the xml.
