# Damage Numbers Off

## What it does

Removes the small damage numbers, so if your in-game settings are "hide others' damage skins" and you are using a damage skin yourself, you will only see your own damage numbers.

Note: This will also hide the damage numbers of enemies hitting you or other players. This includes the burn from Glenn.

### How it's made

Remove the part of the `.dds` image file containing the numbers from each interface color's file.
